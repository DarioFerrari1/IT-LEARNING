## Free learning credentials
Track free course certificates/badges here, but clearly label them as course completion credentials rather than professional certifications.

## Rules
- Do not collect certificates just to fill the CV.
- Prioritize practical skills, projects and professional IT experience.
- Before paying for an exam, check current employer demand and whether it fits the target role.

## Evidence
Store certificate/badge links and dates here. Do not upload sensitive personal documents.
